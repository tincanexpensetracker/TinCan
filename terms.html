What TINCAN is

TINCAN is a personal savings, budgeting, and expense-tracking tool. It helps you record income and expenses, plan monthly budgets, set savings goals, track deposits and plans in the Savings Planner, schedule bill reminders, see forecasts from the on-device AI Coach, back up to your own Google Drive, and optionally save together with others in shared groups. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.
