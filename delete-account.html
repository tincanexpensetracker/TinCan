Delete your account & data

This page explains how to permanently delete your TINCAN account and all associated data. It applies to accounts created with Google Sign-In or with an email address and password. Developer: TINCAN (app package tincan.ai).

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

If you have the app installed:

  • 1. Open TINCAN and sign in.
  • 2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • 3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately and permanently.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app

You can ask us to delete your account even if you no longer have the app installed. Email customercare@tincan.anonaddy.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject “Delete my TINCAN account”. We verify your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Delete only some of your data (keep your account)

You don’t have to delete your whole account to remove data. In the app you can delete individual items at any time while keeping your account:

  • Delete any transaction, budget, recurring payment, piggy bank, or custom category by opening the item and choosing delete.
  • Leave a shared / family group to remove yourself (and your shared entries) from that group.
  • Remove a friend connection at any time.

These changes take effect immediately and sync to your account. If you’d like help removing specific data, email customercare@tincan.anonaddy.com.

Delete your entire account & all data

  • Your profile, profile picture, and sign-in record
  • All transactions, budgets, recurring payments, piggy banks, and custom categories
  • Your streak history and friends connections
  • Your membership in any shared / family groups (other members’ data is unaffected)
Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We do not retain backups of deleted account data beyond what is required to meet legal obligations (for example, fraud prevention); any such residual data is removed on our standard backup-rotation schedule. Data you created in guest mode is stored only on your device and is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its data — there is nothing on our servers to delete.
